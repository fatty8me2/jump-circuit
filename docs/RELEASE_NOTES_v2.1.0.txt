Jump Circuit 2.1: the Flow update.

Four new momentum courses in a new Flow section: Aurora Run, Sky Surf, Hyperloop and Lotus Drift. Chain speed rings, grind rails, carve half-pipes and fly launch arcs at up to 30 m/s.

Keep your speed up to fill the flow meter and enter ZEN: the view opens up, the world goes quiet and the music soars.

Easy to finish on the safe line, hard to master on the flow line. Gold needs near-perfect flow.

Flow courses unlock as you beat campaign courses. Each has its own music, sounds, medals and a Silver hat.
