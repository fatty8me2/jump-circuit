Jump Circuit 2.0 is the big one.

8 new worlds slot into the campaign by difficulty, each with its own music and sounds. A new obstacle kit adds launch barrels, ziplines, flippers, drawbridges and more.

Party mode is rebuilt: CPU racers, party vs CPU, 7 new items, cups with a podium, and new modes King of the Hill, Elimination, Coin Rush and Hot Potato.

New looks: 5 characters, 18 hats, paints, trails, finishes, emotes and victory poses.

Race your personal-best ghost, and chase 3 challenges per course.

Controls change: the D-pad now plays emotes. Move with the left stick or WASD.
